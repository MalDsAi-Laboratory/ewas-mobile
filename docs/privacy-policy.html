🌿 ScrapIt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  |  MalDsAi Laboratory

Overview

ScrapIt ("we", "our", or "us") is an e-waste recycling marketplace that connects sellers with certified recyclers across India. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

By using the ScrapIt app (package: com.ewaste.ewas), you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and user ID, collected at registration.
  • Location Approximate — used to show nearby recyclers and calculate pickup distances. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Photos — images of e-waste items you upload when creating a listing. Stored securely on our servers.
  • Device identifiers — standard Android identifiers used for crash reporting and analytics.
  • Usage data — actions within the app (e.g. orders placed, bids made) to improve the service.

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match sellers with nearby recyclers and facilitate transactions.
  • To process orders, bids, and pickup scheduling.
  • To send order status notifications.
  • To improve app reliability and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name from Google. We use this only to create or identify your ScrapIt account. We do not access your Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google services.

Data Sharing

  • Recyclers — when you place an order, your name, address, and item details are shared with the assigned recycler to arrange pickup.
  • Service providers — we use Amazon Web Services (AWS) for storage. Data is stored in India.
  • Legal compliance — we may disclose data if required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us. Order records may be retained for up to 3 years for legal and tax purposes.

Permissions We Request

  • Camera / Storage — to take or select photos of your e-waste items.
  • Location (foreground only) — to show recyclers near you. Never accessed in the background.
  • Internet — to communicate with our servers.

Children's Privacy

ScrapIt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data collection at any time.

To exercise these rights, email us at vashish39@gmail.com.

Security

We use HTTPS (TLS) for all data in transit and industry-standard encryption for data at rest. Passwords are hashed using BCrypt and never stored in plain text. We do not store payment card details.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.
